# Legacy import parity: raw register and historical quantities

The web restore dispatch invokes the SQLite importer in replaceExisting mode. Android does not delete an existing workspace implicitly. It activates the imported source only into an empty, unenrolled workspace.

- Historical debit and credit are the original register, not reconstructed journal legs. Account statements, groups, category summaries and trial balances use the raw sides of imported posted entries, including a documented mixed-side row. New journals keep their explicit double-entry legs.
- Unknown-currency rows retain the source currency reference and stay historical_unposted; do not use the web fallback that silently maps them to the base currency.
- The invoice-derived quantity is the web bundle formula: type 3/9/4 adds and 2/7/5 subtracts quantity times the original unit factor plus bonus. It is computed by source item and warehouse and preserves fractional historical values. It is not a certified opening count, excludes transfers in this projection, and must not authorize stock posting. When a warehouse is approved it is excluded from the remaining unapproved invoice projection. The historical total and old catalog cost are shown in the opening screen as reference only.
- Transfer headers/lines, offer headers/lines, deleted-document headers/lines and alerts are now also stored as structured historical source collections for newly activated backups, alongside the byte-exact source archive. No extra legacy records are replayed as operational transactions.
- The native report fallback displays derived historical stock quantities when the movement-report service is unavailable; detailed movement and cashbox reconciliation still require a separate implementation. No financial data or user-specific fixture is included in the repository.

The synthetic tests check raw side totals, exclusion of unknown currency, multi-warehouse conversion, bonus, fractional historical lines, preservation of source records and prevention of duplicate opening. Existing already-activated workspaces recover computed quantities from their persisted invoice archive; reclassification of previously excluded mixed-side documents and recovery of extra structured tables from older imports are not automatic.
